From Click to Commission to Payout

Recruit → Share → Track → Calculate → Hold → Pay

  1. Recruit

    Affiliates join through a simple signup flow.

  2. Share

    Every affiliate gets unique links for your store, products, and pages.

  3. Track

    Visits and referred orders are tracked on your own site.

  4. Calculate

    Commissions are calculated per order item.

  5. Hold

    Commissions stay pending until your hold period ends.

  6. Pay

    Create payout batches, mark them paid, and export CSV records.
