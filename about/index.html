Why we built it

— The people who already love your products are your best sales channel.

Customers, creators, and partners recommend products every day. FlyAffiliate gives store owners a simple way to reward them for it — without signing up for an outside affiliate network.

It runs directly on WordPress. Affiliates get a link, sales are tracked to WooCommerce orders, commissions follow clear rules, and payouts stay organized — with every record kept on your own site.
